# When the connection drops

## Nothing is lost
advance_on: continue

Your sessions keep running on the server. The page tells you it's reconnecting and picks them up where they are. Nothing you typed is sent again.

## If a process died
advance_on: continue

If an agent's process has ended, Deck offers to resume it, retry, or start fresh. It stops trying on its own after two attempts in ten minutes, so a failing agent can't spend your plan unattended.
