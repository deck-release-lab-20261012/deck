# A long job while I'm away from my desk

## Sessions run on the server
advance_on: continue

A session is a real terminal on the Deck machine. Your browser is just looking at it. Close the tab, close the laptop, lose the wifi: the agent keeps working.

## Open the same session on your phone
advance_on: continue
primary: Show the address
action: show-address

Open Deck's address on your phone and sign in. The side panel lists the same projects and sessions. Pick one, and you're looking at the same terminal. The key row above the keyboard gives you Escape, Tab, Control and the arrows.

## Take control, or just watch
advance_on: continue

Only one browser types at a time. If the laptop still has the session, the phone shows Take control. Press it to type from the phone; the laptop goes back to watching.
